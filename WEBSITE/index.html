100% Rust Audio & Jitter Engine

16,000 Hz wideband mono PCM & Opus pipeline. Employs an RFC 3550 circular jitter buffer (20ms–60ms), 0.78× exponential decay Packet Loss Concealment (PLC), and 120ms VAD hangover window eliminating consonant clipping.
